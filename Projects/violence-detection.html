Violence Detection in Text

Machine-learning and deep-learning approaches for detecting violent and aggressive narratives.

Overview

This research explores classification approaches for identifying violence-related language in textual narratives, with emphasis on evaluation, data quality, and responsible interpretation.

Methodology overview

Dataset construction and validation; preprocessing; baseline models; neural architectures; transformer fine-tuning; model evaluation and error analysis.
