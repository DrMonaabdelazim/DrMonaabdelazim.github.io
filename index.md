I am an **Assistant Professor, NLP Engineer, and Applied AI Researcher** working at the intersection of Natural Language Processing, Arabic language technologies, speech AI, and human-centered intelligent systems.

My work combines research, engineering, teaching, and technology leadership—with a focus on turning AI research into practical solutions that serve people.

## Research Interests

- **Natural Language Processing:** Arabic NLP, language understanding, text classification, and conversational systems.
- **Speech & Audio AI:** Arabic speech recognition, speech processing, speech synthesis, and speech-to-speech technologies.
- **Applied AI & LLMs:** Large language models, intelligent agents, multimodal AI, and practical AI applications.
- **Human-Centered AI:** AI applications in education, healthcare, and socially meaningful challenges.

## Selected Projects

### Robot Shams — AI-powered Humanoid Nurse
Technical team lead for Robot Shams, a humanoid robotic nurse integrating Arabic speech technologies, conversational AI, and human-centered interaction.

### Arabic Speech & Language Technologies
Research and development across Arabic speech recognition, speech processing, synthesis, and language technologies for Arabic users and communities.

### Applied NLP & Machine Learning
Research projects applying NLP, machine learning, and deep learning to real-world problems, including text analysis and classification.

### AI for Education
Exploring practical AI solutions, learning technologies, and intelligent systems that support accessible, engaging, and personalized learning.
